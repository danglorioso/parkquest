## What it is

A web and mobile app built for people who take the parks seriously. Log the parks you've been to, document your trips, and work your way toward the legendary Sixty-Four.

**Features**
- Interactive map of all 64 National Parks
- Personal passport — every visit, stamped
- Journal with notes, photos, and trip details
- Badge system across five achievement tiers
- Trip planning with friends
- Social feed to share and follow along
