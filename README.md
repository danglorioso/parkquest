# ParkQuest
Created by [Dan Glorioso](https://danglorioso.com).

**Chase all 63. Leave your mark.**

ParkQuest is your companion for exploring every U.S. National Park — track visits, earn badges, journal your adventures, and plan what's next.

---

## What it is

A web and mobile app built for people who take the parks seriously. Log the parks you've been to, document your trips, and work your way toward the legendary Sixty-Three.

**Features**
- Interactive map of all 63 National Parks
- Personal passport — every visit, stamped
- Journal with notes, photos, and trip details
- Badge system across five achievement tiers
- Trip planning with friends
- Social feed to share and follow along
